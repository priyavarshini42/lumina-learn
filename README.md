# 🎓 Vidya AI – Personal AI Teacher

> **"Every student deserves a personal AI teacher."**

**Vidya AI** is an AI-powered multilingual learning platform designed to provide every student with a **24/7 personalized AI mentor**. Built for **IDEA2IMPACT 2026 – Sustainability & Social Impact**, the platform empowers rural and underserved students with accessible, affordable, and personalized education.

---

## 🌱 IDEA2IMPACT 2026

**Theme:** Sustainability & Social Impact

Vidya AI addresses one of society's biggest challenges—**equal access to quality education**—by bringing an intelligent AI teacher into every student's pocket.

---

# 📌 Problem Statement

Millions of students in rural and underserved communities face significant educational barriers, including:

- 👨‍🏫 Shortage of qualified teachers
- 🌍 Language barriers
- 📖 Lack of personalized learning
- ❓ Limited access to doubt-solving
- 🚀 No career or skill guidance
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Limited parent involvement
- 💡 Low student motivation
- 📱 Digital divide between urban and rural education

These challenges contribute to lower academic performance, higher dropout rates, and fewer career opportunities.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

**Vidya AI** is an intelligent AI-powered personal teacher that delivers personalized learning experiences anytime, anywhere.

Instead of replacing teachers, Vidya AI works alongside them by providing every student with a patient, multilingual learning companion available 24/7.
